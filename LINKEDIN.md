## Second Post (Content / Thought Leadership)

```
Most enterprise AI projects fail at the same point.

Not the model. Not the data. The integration.

You can have the best LLM in the world, but if it can't read from your claims system, write to your CRM, trigger a workflow in your ERP, and do all of that in a way your compliance team can audit — it isn't useful yet.

Agentic AI solves this, but only when it's built properly.

Three things we always define before writing a line of code:
1. What is the agent allowed to do autonomously vs. what requires human approval?
2. How do we log every decision for auditability?
3. What happens when the agent is wrong?

The third question is the one most projects skip.
```
