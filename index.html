01 — What we build

Agentic software,
four shapes.

Factiv is a Germany-based software studio focused on agentic applications — AI systems that don't just answer questions, they take actions, run processes, and operate autonomously inside real business workflows.

We work with teams in regulated SMB, institutional due-diligence, and consumer fintech who need AI that is reliable, auditable, and production-grade. Not demos. Not pilots. Software that runs.

01

Agentic workflow automation

End-to-end autonomous processes that replace manual work — voice intake, document extraction, multi-step decision flows, compliance checks. We've shipped these in regulated SMB and institutional settings.

02

Agent architecture & integration

Connecting LLMs, vector stores, tools, and APIs into coherent systems that sit inside your existing infrastructure — without becoming the most fragile thing in the stack. DSGVO- and EU-AI-Act-aware by default.

03

Custom agent development

Bespoke agents with memory, tool use, and reasoning — built to your domain, your data, your audit trail. We've done it across three different verticals; we know what changes and what doesn't.

04

Strategy & enablement

For teams who know they need agentic AI but don't know where to start. We do scoped discovery, architecture sketching, and small-team enablement. Usually one to three weeks.

04 — About Factiv

A studio of two.
Built in Germany.

Factiv is two people.

Donald Vlahovic has spent the last few years building agentic AI products inside fast-moving organisations — most recently at neuroflash. Founded Factiv because the agentic shift demanded a place to build the systems he couldn't ship inside a single product company. Owns architecture, code, and operations.

Bernard Peranic spent 25 years in DACH commercial work, deep in healthcare and adjacent verticals. He owns the customer side at Factiv — discovery, scoping, onboarding, and the human conversation that has to happen before any code is written.

We work as a studio because two people who actually build can outship five who present. We work in Germany because the regulated, DSGVO-first, EU-data-plane requirements aren't a constraint — they're the design.

DSGVO + EU AI Act by design
