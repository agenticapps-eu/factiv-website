01 — What We Do

We Build Agents

Factiv is a Berlin-based software consultancy specialising in agentic applications — AI systems that don't just answer questions, they take actions, run processes, and operate autonomously inside real business workflows.

04 — About Factiv

Built in Berlin.
Built for Scale.

Factiv was founded in 2026 by developers who spent years building AI products inside fast-moving organisations — and grew frustrated with how slowly the technology was actually reaching the industries that needed it most.

We believe agentic AI is the most significant shift in enterprise software in a generation — but only if it's built properly. That means production-grade code, rigorous testing, clear auditability, and an honest conversation about what AI can and can't do.

We work with a small number of clients at a time. That's deliberate. We'd rather build one thing that works perfectly than five things that almost work.

Privacy-First by Default

We build on GDPR-compliant infrastructure hosted in Germany and Switzerland. Your data stays in Europe, always.

German Engineering Standards

We document everything, test everything, and hand over systems your team can actually maintain. No black boxes.

Domain-Specific Expertise

Insurance and finance are our core verticals. We understand regulatory constraints, data sensitivities, and the approval processes that come with them.
